QHow do I know if my basement or crawlspace needs waterproofing?

Look for warning signs like musty odors, water stains, efflorescence (white mineral deposits), peeling paint, or cracks in walls and floors. If you notice bowed or leaning walls, it’s critical to get professional help immediately. We provide thorough inspections to identify current or potential water issues before they worsen.

QWhat’s the difference between interior and exterior waterproofing?

Interior waterproofing manages water that has already entered your basement by installing drainage systems and sump pumps to divert water safely away. Exterior waterproofing prevents water from ever reaching your foundation by applying waterproof membranes and installing drainage on the outside walls. Often, a combination of both methods offers the best protection.

QCan you explain crawlspace encapsulation and its benefits?

Crawlspace encapsulation seals your crawlspace with a durable vapor barrier that blocks moisture from the ground. This prevents mold and mildew growth, reduces humidity, stabilizes your home’s foundation, and improves the indoor air quality throughout your house.

Yes. ARD Waterproofing not only installs sump pumps and drainage systems but also services and repairs them to ensure they operate efficiently when you need them most.
